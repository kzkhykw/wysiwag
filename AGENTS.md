Use the [WYSIWAG verification skill](.agents/skills/wysiwag-verify/SKILL.md) to start, drive, inspect, and capture evidence from the app or CLI. Run `.agents/skills/wysiwag-verify/scripts/verify.py list` to choose a capability and `.agents/skills/wysiwag-verify/scripts/verify.py all` for the existing local verification suite. In a restricted workspace, add `--swift-no-sandbox` before the subcommand. The [feature map](.agents/skills/wysiwag-verify/feature-map.md) is the maintained source of capability coverage and known limits. Keep evidence outside tracked snapshots, use synthetic fixtures and loopback services, and never read credential values or environment files.
